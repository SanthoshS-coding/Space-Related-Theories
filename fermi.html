The Fermi Paradox

"Where is everybody?"-Enrico Fermi

What is the Paradox?

Our galaxy, THe Milky Way, contains around 100 to 400 billion stars. Many of these stars are billions of years older than our sun. If Earth is not completely unique,Then intelligent life should have developed on other planets a long time ago.

Even with slow rocket travel, a civilization should have colonized the entire galaxy by now. But when we look into deep space, we see zero signs of alien technology or even radio signals

Main Explanations

  • The Great Filter: There is a hard wall in evolution that almost no species can survive(like a nuclear war, climate disaster, or asteroid impact).
  • The Zoo Hypothesis:Aliens exist, but they intentionally watch us from a distance and avoid contacting us so we develop naturally.
  • We are early: Maybe Earth is genuinely the very first civilization in our galaxy to reach this level.
